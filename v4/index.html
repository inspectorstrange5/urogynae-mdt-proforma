NG123 checks

    Data set drawn from NICE NG123 (2019, updated). Decision support only; it does not replace clinical judgement or the current guidance. No patient identifiers are collected, and nothing entered is stored or sent anywhere. "Save file" keeps a copy on your device so the referral can be reopened at the MDT to add the outcome. Flow and voiding thresholds (Qmax <15 ml/s, PVR >100 ml) are common clinical cut-offs, not NICE figures.
