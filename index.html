How to use. Fields marked * form the suggested minimum data set (MDS). Sections appear as they become relevant to the presenting problem and proposed treatment. The panel on the right checks the case against NICE NG123 as you type. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere: use Save draft to keep a file locally, or Generate MDT summary to print or copy into your MDT list.
